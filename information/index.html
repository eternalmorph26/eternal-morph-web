About the Team & Engineering Vision

Eternal-Morph is an independent innovation collective uniting talent across Mechanical, Electrical-Electronics, and Software Engineering to build Digital Twin and simulation-driven advanced R&D systems.

ENGINEERING METHODOLOGY & WORK CULTURE

End-to-End Integrated Engineering & Digital Twin Approach

We break mechanical, embedded hardware, and autonomous software layers out of traditional isolated silos, uniting them in a fully integrated R&D and simulation loop.

01 / CONCURRENT LOOP

Interdisciplinary Integrated Design

Mechanical CAD modeling, embedded PCB design, and ROS2 software architecture are not treated as disconnected stages; they are developed concurrently from day one over shared interface standards (URDF, CAN-Bus, ISO flanges).

Zero Integration Mismatch Concurrent CAD-PCB-Firmware Design System-Level Pre-Validation
02 / MODEL-BASED SIM

Model-Based Digital Twin Verification

To minimize physical manufacturing costs and risks, inertia tensors, aerodynamic drag coefficients, and joint torques are verified in Gazebo & Isaac Sim using high-precision mathematical physics engines.

Gazebo & Isaac Sim Dynamic Verification ANSYS FEA/CFD Flow & Stress Analysis Parametric URDF & Xacro Tree
03 / RAPID HARDWARE

HIL Co-Simulation & Rapid Prototyping

Mathematical models validated in simulation are prepared for field deployment by testing STM32 embedded control boards, CAN-Bus telemetry lines, and CNC-machined composite airframes in a Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) cycle.

STM32 & CAN-Bus Real-Time Testing Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Cycle Open-Source CAD & ROS2 Packages
DEVELOPMENT PIPELINE

3-Stage Prototyping Process

Our verifiable engineering roadmap from virtual simulation to physical manufacturing.

01

Digital Twin & Simulation (Active)

SolidWorks / Fusion 360 CAD models are exported to URDF/Xacro format and thoroughly simulated in Gazebo or Isaac Sim for mass, aerodynamics, and joint physics.

Current Active Stage
02

HIL Co-Simulation (Targeted)

Our custom STM32 control boards connect to the simulation server via CAN-Bus/UART, enabling real-time hardware and digital twin co-execution.

Upcoming Phase 2
03

Physical Prototyping & Field Tests

Simulation-verified designs move into CNC machining, 3D printing, carbon-fiber layup, and custom PCB fabrication for real-world field testing.

Final Field Stage

Layer 1: Mechanical & Aerodynamic Architecture

SolidWorks & Fusion 360 Parametric CAD and CFD

The physical chassis, centers of mass, and joint kinematics of our projects are parametrically modeled in CAD. After completing aerodynamic flow and structural stress analyses in ANSYS, models are exported to the simulation layer in URDF/Xacro format.
