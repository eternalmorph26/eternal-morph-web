MORPHOROBOT

Our hybrid robotic platform combining rotary-wing VTOL flight with high-torque 4-wheel terrain driving in a single chassis. Its SolidWorks CAD model is undergoing aerodynamic and ground traction tests in Gazebo.

Digital Twin Dynamic Mode Switcher:
DEFAULT / PARK MODE (SIM) System in nominal standby state. All joint angles are locked at the 0.0 rad nominal reference position.
Parametric CAD & URDF: SolidWorks / Fusion 360 mass and inertia matrices
Embedded Hardware: STM32H7 microcontroller and CAN-Bus driver architecture
Simulation & Control: ROS 2 Humble nodes and VTOL mode-transition controllers

UR10 BIONIC ROBOT HAND
